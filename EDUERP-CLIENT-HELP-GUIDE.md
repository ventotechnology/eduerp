## 2. In-App Contextual Help Drawer

While working in your institution's portal (e.g. `https://eduerp.us/dims/dashboard` or `https://eduerp.us/demo-college/finance`):
1. Look at the bottom of the left-hand navigation sidebar.
2. Click **Need Help?**.
3. A slide-over drawer will open showing articles and FAQs relevant to the specific page you are currently viewing.
4. You can search the full knowledge base or click **Create Support Ticket** directly from the drawer without losing your page context.

## 3. Submitting & Tracking Support Tickets

### Creating a Ticket:
1. Navigate to **[`/support/tickets/new`](https://eduerp.us/support/tickets/new)**.
2. Select your affected module (e.g., *Online Admission*, *Fees & Accounting*, *Examination*).
3. Choose the appropriate priority:
   - **Critical / Urgent:** Immediate operational blockage affecting live students/parents.
   - **High / Normal:** Feature assistance, report formatting, or configuration questions.
4. Provide a clear description and attach screenshots (PNG, JPG, PDF, max 10MB).
5. Submit your ticket to receive a tracking number (e.g., `TKT-2026-000018`).

### Two-Way Messaging & Resolution:
- You can reply to support agents directly from your ticket view (`/support/tickets/[ticketNumber]`).
- When the support team marks the ticket **RESOLVED**, you can either:
  - **Confirm Resolution** and rate your support experience (1–5 stars).
  - **Reopen Ticket** if you require further assistance on the same issue.

## 4. EduERP Training Academy & Certifications

1. Visit **[`/training`](https://eduerp.us/training)** to browse certified curricula tailored to your role:
   - *EduERP Getting Started & Core Fundamentals*
   - *Institution Administrator Essentials*
   - *Admission Officer Training*
   - *Teacher & Classroom Educator Training*
   - *Finance & Accounts Officer Training*
2. Complete lessons and review multimedia guides.
3. Pass the end-of-course assessment (80% passing score).
4. Receive a verifiable digital credential with cryptographic certificate number (e.g., `CERT-TRN-2026-000001`) shareable on resumes and institutional accreditation audits.
5. Verify certificates publicly at [`https://eduerp.us/verify/training/[certificateNumber]`](https://eduerp.us/verify/training).
